# Reasoning Constitution

Status: **IMMUTABLE — explicit approval is required for changes.**

## Mission and boundary

Collectors collect, knowledge remembers, learning improves, and reasoning evaluates. The reasoning layer never predicts and never executes. It transforms governed evidence and context into exactly one of `BUY_SETUPS_ONLY`, `SELL_SETUPS_ONLY`, `WAIT`, or `NO_OPINION`.

## Inputs

Every reasoning path references a timestamped market state, expiring evidence snapshot, versioned knowledge revisions, explicit historical similarities, research artifacts, and approval-required learning suggestions. Learning proposals are exposed only as research context and improvement candidates; they cannot change production behavior.

Only current `VALIDATED` or `INSTITUTIONAL_RULE` knowledge contributes to reasoning reliability. Observation, hypothesis, deprecated, and review-expired knowledge remains visible but cannot establish institutional context.

## Required chain

Every output contains all nine ordered stages:

1. Macro
2. Bias
3. Location
4. Liquidity
5. Momentum
6. SMC
7. Contradictions
8. Trade quality
9. Recommendation

A directional output cannot jump from MACD or another indicator directly to a recommendation. Missing reliable support in any required evidence stage downgrades the result to `WAIT`. Missing current validated knowledge or an expired market state downgrades it to `NO_OPINION`.

## Institutional questions

The output answers what is happening, why, what supports it, what weakens it, how reliable it is, what is missing, what alternative explanations exist, which evidenced historical contexts are similar, what would invalidate the conclusion, and what would improve it.

Language describes evidence preference, never certainty. The engine may say current evidence favors BUY attention; it may never claim price will rise.

Reasoning reliability is reproducible: Version 4 evidence confidence is multiplied by the average geometric combination of reliability and confidence across current validated knowledge objects. The formula is a hypothesis subject to research and policy versioning.

## Permanent reasoning memory

Every full input snapshot and resulting reasoning path is appended to the reasoning audit. Later mistakes are separate append-only objects with kind, description, evidence references, and a corrective hypothesis. Original paths are never rewritten.
