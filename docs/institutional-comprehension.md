# Institutional Comprehension

Gold Brain augments professional judgment; it does not ask the trader to defer to opaque machinery.
Every new official decision requires a review tied to the exact reasoning ID.

An approved review provides a plain-language summary, translates institutional abstractions,
explains every numerical claim with a calculation reference, identifies no unresolved opaque
elements, and states both where trader judgment remains necessary and where automation stops.
Reasoning with an unexplained latent factor, abstract score, statistical transformation, or hidden
dependency is rejected rather than simplified after it has already influenced production.

`OfficialDecision 5.0` records the approved comprehension-review ID. A rejected review, a review for
another reasoning path, missing concept explanations, or missing statistic explanations cannot be
authorized.

Readability automation may flag jargon but cannot certify professional meaning. Production requires
reviewer identity, durable review storage, freshness/hash binding to the reasoning artifact, and a
controlled vocabulary whose definitions professional traders can inspect.
