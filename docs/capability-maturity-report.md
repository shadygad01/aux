# Capability Maturity Report

Every capability occupies exactly one maturity stage. Maturity describes lifecycle position;
readiness records evidence at a milestone.

| Capability | Maturity stage | Readiness | Promotion evidence missing |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Collection | Foundation | 40 | Production adapters and source assurance |
| Normalization | Foundation | 40 | Published transformation contract |
| Evidence | Implementation | 60 | Canonical lineage and reliability validation |
| Knowledge | Implementation | 60 | Restart rehydration and freshness validation |
| Reasoning | Foundation | 40 | Canonical Market Story and parity evidence |
| Decision | Foundation | 40 | Canonical Market Thesis and single scoring path |
| Learning | Implementation | 60 | Durable records and outcome calibration |
| Research | Architecture | 40 | Research Finding and executed validation program |
| Publishing | Architecture | 20 | Decision Presentation implementation and contract |
| Monitoring | Architecture | 20 | Executable readiness, durable governance, and alerts |

Allowed stages are Research, Architecture, Foundation, Implementation, Validation, Production,
Optimization, and Institutional. Stage changes require a history record and may move backward when
evidence expires or a blocker is discovered.
