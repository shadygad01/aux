# Macro Capability

The **Macro Capability** is the single canonical entry point for all institutional macro intelligence (DXY Dollar Strength, US10Y/US02Y Treasury Yields, High Impact News Environment, and PDH/PDL/PWH/PWL/PMH/PML Liquidity Reference Levels).

## Mission

- Collect free, provenance-bearing macro facts.
- Produce canonical `MacroContext` and `MacroAssessment`.
- Modify decision confidence or force fail-closed `WAIT` on high-impact unresolved news without bypassing SMC technical structure.
