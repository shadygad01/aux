# H-028 supplied five-year replay

## Production decision

Reject H-028 as formulated. Do not connect it to the live decision policy.

## Data audit

- M5: 262,656 supplied bars, 2021-08-11 through 2026-08-07 UTC.
- M15: 87,552 supplied bars and exact OHLC/volume lineage from M5.
- H1: inconsistent coverage (ends 2026-07-31), 14,162 zero-volume flat bars,
  and 3,709 price mismatches versus overlapping M5 aggregation.
- Canonical research lineage therefore uses M5 only, censors 17,573
  zero-volume flat M5 bars, and rebuilds 81,690 M15 plus 20,405 H1 bars.
- Prices required an explicit 100,000 divisor.
- Files contain midpoint OHLC rather than executable bid/ask OHLC. Spread,
  commission and slippage are modeled as disclosed point costs; results are
  not tick-executable evidence.

## Frozen hypothesis and split

- H1 supplies premium/discount context only.
- M15 supplies CHoCH within the latest four closed M15 bars.
- M5 must confirm the same direction with established structure and BOS.
- All inputs are fully closed at signal time; consecutive duplicate episodes
  are suppressed.
- Discovery ends 2024-08-03, followed by a five-day embargo.
- Holdout is 2024-08-08 through 2026-08-08.
- One open trade at a time and at most three trades per day.
- Base modeled cost is 0.15 gold points per side. Stress uses 0.225 points per
  side and one additional M5 bar of manual delay.
- Historical macro is excluded because no point-in-time macro series was supplied.

## Result

- 549 signals: 353 discovery and 196 holdout.
- 131 executed holdout trades after portfolio constraints.
- The discovery search did not produce a plan profitable in every discovery
  year. The least-bad plan was the registered 50% partial at 2R with a 0.5 ATR trail.
- Holdout net: +7.28R, but expectancy was only +0.0556R and its bootstrap 95%
  lower bound was -0.1838R.
- Profit factor: 1.087, below the 1.20 requirement.
- Holdout halves: +10.20R then -2.92R, failing temporal stability.
- BUY: 52 trades, below the 60-trade minimum; CI lower bound -0.268R.
- SELL: 79 trades, +1.09R, PF 1.021, CI lower bound -0.295R.
- At 0.60 points per side the result becomes -3.06R with 17.03R drawdown.
- Low-ATR holdout regime: two trades, both losses. High-ATR PF was only 1.096.

The positive aggregate is not robust evidence of edge. The statistical,
directional-sample, cost-sensitivity and time-stability gates reject the
hypothesis independently.
