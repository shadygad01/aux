# H-028 bottom-up reversal: data-coverage smoke test

## Decision

Do not promote H-028. The available archive is far below the frozen acceptance
minimum and the only two non-duplicate signals both stopped out.

## Frozen signal

- H1: context only; BUY requires discount and SELL requires premium.
- M15: CHoCH toward the candidate direction on one of the latest four fully
  closed M15 candles.
- M5: fully closed structure must agree with the candidate and show BOS.
- Signal evaluation: every closed M5 candle; no open candle may enter any
  timeframe window; consecutive bars from the same reversal episode are
  de-duplicated.
- Macro: excluded because no point-in-time historical macro series is present.

runner trail; neither trade reached the partial.

## Acceptance gap

The protocol requires an embargoed untouched 2024-08-08 through 2026-08-08
holdout, at least 60 trades per direction, positive bootstrap lower bound,
profit factor >= 1.20, positive holdout halves, drawdown/streak limits, cost and
delay stress, neighboring-exit robustness, and regime checks. None can be
claimed from two same-direction smoke signals. Status remains `TESTING`, with
current evidence adverse and insufficient.
